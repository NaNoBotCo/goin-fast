What was computed rather than looked up

Orbital and escape speeds, the Mach cone angle, the drag and power figures, the gamma factors, the energy to accelerate a two-tonne truck, and the five cannonball trajectories were all computed in tools/figures.py from the formulas printed beside them, using G from CODATA[27] and the Earth's mass and radius from the NASA fact sheet[15]. The diagrams are drawn from those same computations, so a figure and the sentence beside it cannot drift apart.
